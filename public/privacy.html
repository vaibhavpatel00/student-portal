Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Vignan Student Portal. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies you as a student of Vignan University, including:

  • Roll Number and Email Address
  • Attendance Records
  • Official Course and Department information

3. Third-Party Advertising (Unity Ads)

Important: Our application integrates Unity Ads, a third-party advertising service. Unity Ads may collect data about your device to provide personalized and relevant advertisements.

The data collected by Unity Ads includes:

  • Device identifiers (IDFA for iOS and GAID for Android).
  • Device metadata (model, OS version, language settings).
  • Your IP address and approximate location.
  • Information about ads shown and your interactions with them.

For more information, please visit the Unity Privacy Policy.

4. Data Usage and Security

Your authentication data is used solely to verify your student status. We implement industry-standard security measures to prevent unauthorized access to your academic records.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact the Vignan University IT department.

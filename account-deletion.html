Account & Data Deletion

Student Portal App
We value your privacy. If you wish to delete your account and all associated personal data from the Student Portal app, you can request data deletion using the button below.

What happens when you delete your account?

  • Your profile information (name, email, etc.) will be permanently erased.
  • Your app preferences and saved data will be removed.
  • Any active sessions will be terminated immediately.
  • This action is irreversible and cannot be undone.

Note: Data deletion requests are typically processed within 3-5 business days. You will receive an email confirmation once your data has been completely removed from our systems.
